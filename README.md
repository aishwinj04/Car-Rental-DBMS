## Introduction
The Car Rental DBMS is designed to organize customer, review, vehicle, reservation, rental, and payment data efficiently and effectively. It also tracks vehicle maintenance to ensure customer safety and satisfaction when making a reservation. Using a DBMS for this scenario is vital to keep track of operations among thousands of customers, keep the integrity of the data, and avoid redundancy. 

## Purpose
This report provides an overview of the Car Rental Database Management System (DBMS). It explores the business nature of the database, conceptual and internal designs, normalization of the relations, and the relational algebra notation of our queries. In addition, it describes possibilities for future development and sets out expectations for the system to ensure a good experience for the customers that make use of this service.
